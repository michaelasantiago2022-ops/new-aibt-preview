WHO WE ARE

About Adelaide Institute of Business and Technology

As a member of Adelaide Education Group, AIBT is a Registered Training Organisation delivering nationally recognised vocational education and training across Adelaide and Melbourne.

Since welcoming its first students in 2010, AIBT has focused on practical, industry-relevant education that equips learners with knowledge, skills and confidence to pursue employment, career development and further study.
