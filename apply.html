ENTRY REQUIREMENTS

Understand your entry pathway

Entry requirements depend on the qualification and applicant's prior study or experience. AIBT may consider previous study, equivalent qualifications and relevant work or life experience, subject to course-specific criteria.

Applicants may be required to complete a Language, Literacy and Numeracy (LLN) assessment before enrolment.

  • Academic transcripts or certificates
  • Evidence of relevant work or life experience, where applicable
  • English language evidence, where applicable
  • Any additional course-specific documents
