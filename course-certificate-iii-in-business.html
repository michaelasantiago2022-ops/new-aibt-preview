Certificate III in Business

This qualification reflects the role of individuals in a variety of business services job roles. It is especially relevant to individuals who are establishing their own approach to work and their own performance standards. Students develop the technology and business skills needed to carry out routine procedural, clerical, administrative and operational tasks.

WHY STUDY BUSINESS?

Build the skills used in today's workplaces

The Certificate III in Business is designed for people starting out in business services or developing the practical skills needed to work effectively in an office or administrative environment.

01

Workplace communication

Practise communicating with colleagues and contributing to a team environment.

02

Digital business skills

Develop capability in business software, electronic presentations, spreadsheets and digital communication.

03

Everyday business operations

Learn to prepare documents, identify business risks and support safe, inclusive and sustainable work practices.

ENTRY & ADMISSIONS

Entry requirements

Applicants must meet the academic requirements and, where applicable, the English language requirements for this qualification.

Academic requirements

  • Completion of Year 10, or literacy and mathematical skills equivalent to Year 10 in the Australian schooling system.
  • VET for School applicants must be currently studying in Year 10 or above.
  • Upfront Assessment of Need (UAN) applies to out-of-school/adult students where relevant. Contact AIBT for guidance about eligibility.
  • International students under 18 must live in approved homestay accommodation or with an approved guardian.

English language requirements

For international students only:

  • IELTS overall score of 4.5, or an accepted equivalent.

Applicants should contact AIBT if they need advice about accepted English language evidence.

Language, Literacy and Numeracy (LLN) assessment

A Language, Literacy and Numeracy assessment is conducted for all entrants before enrolment. This helps AIBT understand each student's learning needs and provide appropriate support throughout the program.
