COURSE OVERVIEW

About this course

This qualification reflects cooks who use well-developed cookery skills and kitchen operations knowledge to prepare food and menu items. Completion contributes to recognition as a trade cook.

Assessment includes written assignments, reports, quizzes, knowledge tests and presentations, plus practical demonstrations in simulated and real workplace environments. Pathway: Certificate IV in Kitchen Management or equivalent.
