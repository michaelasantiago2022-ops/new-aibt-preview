COURSE OVERVIEW

About this course

This qualification prepares care workers to provide person-centred support in community, home-care and residential settings. A compulsory 120-hour practical placement is required.

ENTRY & ADMISSIONS

Entry requirements

Academic requirements

  • Completion of Australian Year 10 or equivalent
  • VET for School students: current Year 10 or above
  • UAN applies to out-of-school/adult students
  • Physical attributes suitable for placement, including manual handling and assisting clients

English language requirements

IELTS 5.5 overall or equivalent
