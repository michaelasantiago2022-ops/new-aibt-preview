COURSE OVERVIEW

About this course

A combined hospitality pathway designed to build kitchen management and hospitality operations capability, covering food and beverage production, customer service and management.

360 hours of practical work placement and assessment in a commercial hospitality venue are compulsory. Placement is arranged by AIBT; placement hours may be paid. The pathway can progress to an Advanced Diploma of Hospitality Management or a Bachelor’s degree in Hospitality Management.

ENTRY & ADMISSIONS

Entry requirements

Academic requirements

  • Minimum age 17 years
  • Completion of Australian Year 11 or equivalent
  • Physical attributes suitable for placement in the hospitality industry
  • International English: IELTS Academic 5.5, TOEFL PBT 506, TOEFL iBT 62, Cambridge English Advanced 47 or PTE Academic 46
  • Provide proof of up-to-date immunisation, including COVID-19 vaccination record
