COURSE OVERVIEW

About this course

This qualification reflects chefs and cooks with supervisory or team-leading roles in a kitchen, working independently and using discretion to solve non-routine problems.

Assessment includes written assignments, reports, quizzes, knowledge tests and presentations, plus practical demonstrations in simulated and real workplace environments. Pathway: Diploma of Hospitality Management or equivalent.
