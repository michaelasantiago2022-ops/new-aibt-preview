COURSE OVERVIEW

About this course

A structured yet flexible English language program supporting personal, academic and professional goals. Students can start at a level based on placement results, progress at their own pace and exit after completing a module.

Modules: Elementary, Pre-Intermediate, Intermediate, Upper Intermediate and Advanced. Skills include speaking, listening, reading, writing, grammar, pronunciation and vocabulary.
