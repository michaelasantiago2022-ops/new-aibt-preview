COURSE OVERVIEW

About this course

An advanced TESOL and Literacy qualification covering TESOL approaches, curriculum development, applied linguistics, second language acquisition and assessment design, with opportunities to apply knowledge in real TESOL classrooms.

The course supports professional competence in TESOL program planning, teaching and assessment. Learning methodology includes synchronous distance delivery in a real-time virtual classroom and onsite lectures, presentations, group activities, case studies and practical sessions. Assessment includes written work, knowledge tests, role plays and presentations.
