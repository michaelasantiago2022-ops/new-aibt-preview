YOUR NEXT STEP STARTS HERE

Start building your future while you're still at school.

AIBT's VET for School Students courses teach the soft skills students need to succeed, inspire optimism and build entrepreneurial skills. The courses are designed to promote learning, achieve certification and provide a range of pathways.

Alongside weekday classes, AIBT also runs intensive Fast Track classes during school holidays, offering added flexibility for busy students and those from regional areas.

Designed for school students

Applications are made through your school's VET Coordinator or Careers Counsellor. Ask your school about eligibility, available courses and delivery options.
