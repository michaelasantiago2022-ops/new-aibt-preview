COURSE OVERVIEW

About this course

Introduction to IT Support is one of AIBT's stackable VET options for school students.

Stackable VET courses are brief courses with specific objectives. They offer pre-vocational skills, enhance work preparedness and provide an opportunity to explore different fields before committing to a particular qualification.

COURSE CONTENT

Units of competency

The following units are listed for this stackable course on the AIBT website.

  • ICTSAS211 — Develop solutions for basic ICT malfunctions and problems
  • ICTSAS212 — Record the requirements of client support requests
  • ICTICT219 — Interact and resolve queries with ICT clients
  • BSBCRT201 — Develop and apply thinking and problem solving skills
